# Evening hours at the Millbrook Public Library

The trustees of the Millbrook Public Library vote on November 17, 2026 on whether, and how, to extend evening hours. The documents provided are what has been gathered so far: survey results, staff and budget information, correspondence, and reports.

Write a recommendation memo to the trustees. Keep it to about one page (no more than 800 words). It should:

- State a clear recommendation: whether to extend hours, and if so at which location, on which evenings, until what time, starting when, and whether as a trial or a permanent change.
- Give the main reasons for it.
- Estimate the first-year cost and say how it would be paid for.
- Say what has to be in place before the change starts, and name the main risks.
- Point out where the documents disagree, or where a claim doesn't hold up, and say how you weighed it.
- Briefly mention the alternatives you considered and why you didn't choose them.

Back up key facts and figures by naming the document they come from. Write for busy trustees: plain language, no padding.
