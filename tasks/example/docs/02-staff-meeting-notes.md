# Staff Meeting Notes, September 9, 2026

Main Library staff room, 8:30–9:40 am, before opening. Notes: Bea Harmon.

Present: Denise Okafor (Director), Theo Brandt (Circulation Supervisor), Mei Lin Zhou (Reference Librarian), Jess Kowalczyk (Teen Services Librarian), Anjali Mehta (Systems & Data Librarian), Carl Dobbins (Facilities Supervisor), Renata Silva (Library Assistant and Staff Association steward), and 9 other staff. Rosa Delgado (Eastside Branch Manager) joined by phone.

## 1. Evening hours

Denise explained that the trustees want options for longer evening hours at Main in time for their November 17 meeting. The Friends' survey results came in yesterday and show a lot of interest in later weekday hours. Nothing has been decided, and she wants to hear from staff before she writes anything up.

**Availability poll.** Theo polled all 15 public-service staff at Main last week and 14 answered. Five said they could work at least one evening a week on a regular basis:

- Mei Lin: Tuesdays and Wednesdays
- Theo: Thursdays only (he coaches his son's team Tuesday and Wednesday nights)
- Renata: Tuesdays and Thursdays
- Omar Farouk: Mondays and Wednesdays
- Grace Pell: any night Monday to Thursday, but no more than two nights a week

Two more said they would take an occasional evening for extra hours, but not as a standing commitment. Theo pointed out that of the five, only he and Mei Lin are designated persons in charge. Nobody who can be in charge is available on Mondays. Denise said she could cover as person in charge "in a pinch, but not every week."

**Pages.** Theo also noted that three of our six pages are 15, and under state law they can't work past 7 pm on school nights, so evening shelving would fall to the assistants.

**Staff Association.** Renata summarized what the agreement says (Article 14):

- Work after 6 pm is paid a shift differential of $1.75 an hour.
- A current employee's regular schedule can't be changed without 30 days' notice and the employee's consent. Evening work for current staff is voluntary.
- New positions can be posted with evening hours as a condition of the job.

She suggested reposting the two Library Assistant positions (19 hours a week each) that have been vacant since April as evening positions. Denise liked the idea. HR has told her that posting, interviews and background checks usually take 10 to 12 weeks.

**Teen room.** Jess said 3 to 6 pm is by far her busiest and hardest time. She works 9 to 5, so the teen room already has no dedicated staff in the last hour. If hours are extended she would want the teen room either closed at 6 or staffed. "Some of the kids still here at 5:55 are the ones with nowhere else to go. That cuts both ways."

**Parking lot.** Mei Lin said the walk to the rear lot is already uncomfortable in winter and "pitch black by the dumpsters after 7." Carl said some of the pole lights are out and he would do an after-dark walkthrough in early October.

**Eastside.** Rosa asked that Eastside not be forgotten; her patrons keep asking about Saturdays. Denise said she would include a cost for longer Eastside Saturday hours in her memo.
