# Main Library Use by Hour, Spring 2026

Prepared by Anjali Mehta, Systems & Data Librarian, for Denise Okafor. October 12, 2026.

Denise asked for hourly figures to inform the evening hours discussion. All figures are for weekdays from March 2 to May 29, 2026 unless noted.

## Door counts

Average number of people entering the Main Library per hour, Monday to Friday:

| Hour | Visitors |
|---|---|
| 10–11 am | 36 |
| 11 am–12 pm | 49 |
| 12–1 pm | 61 |
| 1–2 pm | 57 |
| 2–3 pm | 60 |
| 3–4 pm | 88 |
| 4–5 pm | 79 |
| 5–6 pm | 31 |

The 5–6 pm figure needs a caution. We announce closing at 5:45, and staff at the door usually turn people away after about 5:50, so the last "hour" is really about 45 minutes, and people who know our hours don't try to come in at 5:40. I wouldn't read it as a measure of how many people would come in the evening.

## Wifi after closing

Our wifi reaches the front steps, the benches along Harlow Street, and most of the rear lot, and people use it after we close. Between 6 and 9 pm on weekdays, an average of 34 unique devices connected each evening: Monday 29, Tuesday 41, Wednesday 44, Thursday 39, Friday 18. About 72% of after-close sessions started between 6 and 8 pm; very few started after 8:30.

These are people using a library service while the library is closed, some of them sitting in their cars in the dark.

## Holds

14% of holds placed in spring 2026 expired without being picked up, compared with 9% in spring 2019. We don't know why. Some of it is probably people who can't get here before 6.

## Fairhaven's experience

Paul Asante, director of the Fairhaven Public Library, shared their figures through the regional consortium. Fairhaven has been open until 9 pm Monday to Thursday since September 2024. Their average visitors: 6–7 pm, 52; 7–8 pm, 38; 8–9 pm, 12. They dropped Monday evenings in January 2026 because use was low, and are now open until 9 on Tuesday to Thursday only. Their circulation rose 3% in the first year of evening hours, about the same as the regional average over that period.

Paul's advice: "Staff the six-to-eight window well. The last hour is mostly for the people who close up the building."

## Computers

Over the same period, Main's 18 public computers were 71% booked between 3 and 6 pm. Eastside's 6 computers were 97% booked in the same hours, with a waiting list most days.
