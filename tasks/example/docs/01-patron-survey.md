# 2026 Patron Survey: Hours and Access

Prepared by the Friends of the Millbrook Library survey committee (Ellen Marsh, chair) for the library's trustees. September 8, 2026.

## How the survey was run

The survey ran from August 3 to August 30, 2026. It was offered online, through a link in the library's monthly email newsletter and on the website, and on paper at the service desks of the Main Library on Harlow Street and the Eastside Branch at Cedar Park Community Center.

We received 612 completed responses: 437 online (71%) and 175 on paper.

We asked which location people use most. 540 (88%) said Main, 55 (9%) said Eastside, and 17 (3%) said both or didn't answer. For comparison, the library's records list Eastside as the home branch for 31% of active cardholders, so Eastside users are clearly under-represented. We also ran out of paper forms at Eastside for about a week (August 17–24), which didn't help.

Only 18 respondents (3%) were under 18. Most (62%) were between 25 and 54.

## Main findings

**Would a change in hours make you visit more often?** (one answer)

| Answer | Responses | Share |
|---|---|---|
| Yes, later closing on weekdays | 355 | 58% |
| Yes, Sunday afternoon hours | 135 | 22% |
| Yes, earlier opening | 67 | 11% |
| No change needed | 55 | 9% |

**If later weekday closing, what closing time?** (355 respondents)

| Closing time | Share |
|---|---|
| 7 pm | 18% |
| 8 pm | 41% |
| 9 pm | 27% |
| Later than 9 pm | 6% |
| No preference | 8% |

**Which evenings?** (choose any) Wednesday 66%, Thursday 64%, Tuesday 61%, Monday 38%, Friday 17%.

**Why later hours?** (choose up to two) "I work until 5 or later and can't get there before closing" 47%; "a quiet place to study or work" 29%; "to pick up holds" 24%; "computer or wifi access" 14%; other 11%.

**Eastside respondents.** Of the 55 people who use Eastside most, the most common request was longer Saturday hours at Eastside (22 people, 40%). Later weekday hours at Main came second (13 people, 24%). The branch currently closes at 2 pm on Saturdays.

## Comments

We received 214 written comments. A few that stood out:

- "I get off the train at 5:40. By the time I'm downtown the doors are locked."
- "Please don't make it a 9 o'clock thing. Nobody is going to the library at 8:45 on a Tuesday. Eight would do it."
- "Sunday! The one day I have free and you're closed."
- "The Eastside branch is the one that needs more hours, not Harlow Street."

## Caveats

This was a voluntary survey promoted mainly to people already on the library's mailing list, so it tells us most about current, regular users. It says little about teenagers, people who don't use the library now, or Eastside residents. We would not treat the percentages as representative of Millbrook as a whole.
