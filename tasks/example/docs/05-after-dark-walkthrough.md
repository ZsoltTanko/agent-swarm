# After-Dark Walkthrough and Incident Review: Main Library

To: Denise Okafor, Director
From: Carl Dobbins, Facilities Supervisor
Date: October 7, 2026

As agreed at the September 9 staff meeting, I walked the Main Library site after dark on Tuesday, October 6, from 6:15 to 7:30 pm, with Mei Lin Zhou. I also went through the incident log for the past twelve months.

## Walkthrough

**Harlow Street entrance.** Well lit. The streetlights and the bus shelter across the street are bright, and the book drop is in plain view of the street. No concerns.

**Rear parking lot.** This is the problem. The lot has 38 spaces and is used by all staff and most patrons who drive. There are eight pole lights. Three are out, and have been for most of the year. The other five are on a mechanical timer that switches them off at 7:00 pm, which was fine when we closed at 6. At 7:05 the lot was dark apart from some spill from the Harlow Tire sign next door. The path from the staff door to the lot runs past the dumpster enclosure and has no light of its own.

Dunmore Electric has quoted $6,800 to replace the three fixtures with LED heads and swap the timer for a photocell with a programmable shut-off, so the lot stays lit until 30 minutes after closing. Lead time is 4 to 6 weeks from the order.

**Staff door.** The camera covers the door but not the lot.

## Incident log, October 1, 2025 – September 30, 2026

There were 17 incident reports at Main:

- 11 between 3 and 6 pm on school days, mostly in the teen room or the lobby: disruptive behavior, two fights, and one case of vandalism in a restroom.
- 3 in the morning or midday: a medical call, a theft from a bag, and a patron who refused to leave.
- 3 at or after closing. Two involved people lingering in the rear lot while staff were leaving. The third, on January 13, 2026, was the most serious: a man who had been asked to leave earlier that afternoon followed a staff member to her car at about 6:10 pm. She got into the car and drove off, and we called the police. She wasn't hurt, but she no longer works closing shifts.

Only the January 13 incident involved the police. The rest were handled by staff.

## Recommendations

1. No extended hours until the rear lot lights are repaired and the timer is replaced.
2. Two-person close, every night: at least two staff in the building at closing, and they leave together. Under Policy 7.3 the person in charge must be on site until the building is locked and the alarm is set, so one of the two has to be the person in charge.
3. On school nights, a third staff member on duty until 7 pm, preferably someone comfortable handling the teen room. Most of our incidents happen in the after-school crowd, and a later closing will stretch that crowd out.
4. A panic button at the main desk, wired to the alarm company (about $400).

None of this is in the current budget. I've sent Denise the Dunmore quote separately.
