# Evening Hours: Options and Costs

To: Finance Committee, Library Trustees
From: Denise Okafor, Library Director
Date: October 1, 2026

The trustees asked for costed options for extending evening hours at the Main Library. This memo sets out three, plus the Eastside question several of you raised, and my recommendation.

## Where we are now

The Main Library is open 47 hours a week: Monday through Friday 10 am to 6 pm, and Saturday 10 am to 5 pm.

Anjali's preliminary door counts show that our 5–6 pm hour is already the quietest of the day, with about 31 people coming in, against 88 between 3 and 4. That makes me cautious about very late closings. On the other hand, the Friends' survey shows real interest in later weekday hours, and I take that seriously.

Theo tells me seven staff have expressed interest in evening shifts, so I believe a modest expansion can be staffed by existing part-time staff taking additional hours at their current rates.

## Assumptions

- Two staff on duty for each added hour: a librarian or supervisor in charge, plus one library assistant. Blended rate $26.30 an hour.
- Building costs (heating and cooling, lights, extra custodial time): about $9 per added open hour.
- Together that is $61.60 per added hour, or about $3,200 a year for each weekly hour added.
- No capital costs are expected.

## Options

| Option | Schedule | Added hours per week | Annual cost | Pilot, Jan 4 – Jun 30, 2027 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | Tue, Wed, Thu until 8 pm | 6 | $19,200 | $9,600 |
| B | Tue and Thu until 8 pm | 4 | $12,800 | $6,400 |
| C | Mon–Thu until 9 pm | 12 | $38,400 | $19,200 |

**Eastside.** Extending Eastside's Saturday hours from 10–2 to 10–5 (3 hours a week) would cost about $9,600 a year on the same assumptions.

## Paying for it

There is no new town money this year. Lorraine Voss has been clear that the Council will not consider a mid-year appropriation.

The two Library Assistant positions that have been vacant since April are carried in the FY27 budget at about $41,800 a year. If we hold them open through December, roughly $20,000 of that will go unspent, which is enough to cover any of the three pilots through June 30.

Longer hours may also affect our state aid. I have been corresponding with Hal Brennan at the State Library about it and will bring the details to all the trustees. I have not counted any of that money here.

## Recommendation

I recommend Option A as a pilot from January 4 through June 30, 2027, paid for from vacancy savings, with a report to the trustees in May on use and cost.

Option C is a big jump for a building whose last hour is already slow, and 9 pm closings are hard on staff. Option B is the cheapest, but it gives patrons only two evenings, and the survey's three most popular evenings were Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday.

I would ask the Finance Committee to endorse Option A so that it can go before all the trustees on November 17.
